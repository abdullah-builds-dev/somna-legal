Somna Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Somna is a sleep and relaxation app provided by YOUR_DEVELOPER_NAME (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and display name, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Sleep check-ins: the mood you choose each morning and the optional note you write.
  • Listening sessions: which sound you listened to, on which night, and for how many minutes. This powers your weekly view and streak.
  • Settings: your bedtime, whether the bedtime reminder is on, and whether gentle haptics are on.
  • On your phone only: your volume level and a local copy of your settings. These stay on your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or microphone.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, show your Journal, streak and history, remember your settings, and play your chosen sounds. Your check-in notes are for you. We do not use them for advertising and we do not share them.

Notifications

If you turn on the bedtime reminder, the app asks permission to send notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your phone. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone settings at any time.

Where your data is stored

Your account and journal data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which acts as our service provider. Google may process data on servers in different countries. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Our setup is designed so that each user can only access their own data.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show advertisements in the app. We do not share your data with third parties except the service providers needed to run the app, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your account and the data linked to it within 30 days.

Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and all data linked to it at any time. Instructions are on our account deletion page, or email YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL from the address you signed up with.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have additional rights under local law. To use any of these rights, email YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Children

Somna is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent to and from the app over encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app.
