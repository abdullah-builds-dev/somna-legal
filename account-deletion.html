Dozli Account Deletion

Dozli users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by following the instructions below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Click the request button below.
  2. Send the email using the email address associated with your Dozli account.
  3. Use the subject "Dozli Account Deletion Request".
  4. Do not include your password.

Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete your Dozli account and associated personal data that we control, including account information and saved journal entries where applicable.

Data retention

Certain information may be retained when required by law or when necessary for security and fraud prevention. Such data will be retained only for the applicable required period.

Deletion timeframe

We aim to process verified requests within 30 days. We will contact you if additional time is needed.

Contact

For account deletion questions, email YOUR_EMAIL@gmail.com.
